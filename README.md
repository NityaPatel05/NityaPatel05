## About

I'm a Computer Science undergrad at **Nirma University** (B.Tech, CGPA 8.88, graduating 2027) based in Ahmedabad, India. I build AI systems that are meant to run in production, not just in a notebook: retrieval pipelines with validated citations, multi-agent workflows that checkpoint and recover, and low-latency ML services.

- Recently interned as an **AI/ML Intern at PARAM Consulting Services**, where I fine-tuned a small Qwen model for tool-calling and built a meeting-audio to minutes-of-meeting pipeline.
- Won **1st place (Skill Mirage)** and a **Top 5 overall** finish at HackNUThon 2025, and was **2nd Runner-up** at the Fintech Hackathon @ PDEU.
- Currently focused on agentic AI, evaluation, and observability for LLM applications.
